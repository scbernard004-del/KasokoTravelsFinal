# Photography sources

Each listing uses a distinct, locally packaged photograph of the named destination or property. New photos replace the processed Ngorongoro landscape, dusk Elewana exterior, Zuri poster and Tanga lounge. No AI imagery or added colour filters are used. Images are resized and compressed for display. Photos retain their source owners’ rights; the source links identify provenance.

See `credits.html` for visible attribution and `photo-sources.json` for the complete URL, author/licence where supplied, dimensions and checksum.
